# the-actual-price-privacy-policy

Privacy Policy for The Actual Price.
Last updated, September 16th, 2026.

This privacy policy describes how The Actual Price, referred to as "the app", "we", or "us", handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect. The Actual Price is designed to calculate sales tax based on your local tax rate. To do this, the app may access your device's location in order to determine the applicable local sales tax rate for your area. We do not store, sell, or share your precise location data with third parties.

If the app includes any account features, purchase history, or preferences, that information is stored locally on your device or, if applicable, in a secure cloud service, and is not sold to third parties.

How We Use Your Information. Location data is used solely to calculate accurate local sales tax rates. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core function of the app.

Data Sharing. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except where required to provide core app functionality, such as tax rate lookup services, or where required by law.

Data Retention. We retain only the information necessary to provide the app's functionality, and for no longer than needed for that purpose.

Your Choices. You can disable location permissions at any time through your device settings, though this may limit the app's ability to calculate local tax rates accurately. You may also delete the app at any time to remove any locally stored data.

Children's Privacy. The Actual Price is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy. We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us. If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at policy@thesimplapps.com
